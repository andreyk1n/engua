Remote Work

Over the past few years, more companies have allowed employees to work from home. Supporters say remote work saves commuting time and helps people focus better. However, some managers worry that employees feel less connected to their teams when they rarely meet in person. Many companies now use a hybrid model, where staff come to the office two or three days a week and work from home the rest of the time. Experts believe this balance may become the standard way of working in the future, rather than a temporary solution.

    • B It saves commuting time
    • C It reduces salaries
    • D It requires more meetings

  2. 47. What concern do some managers have about remote work?

    • A Employees work too much
    • B Employees feel less connected to their teams
    • C Employees earn too little
    • D Employees travel too often

  3. 48. What is a 'hybrid model' according to the passage?

    • A Working only from home
    • B Working only from the office
    • C A mix of office and home days
    • D Working only on weekends

  4. 49. How do experts view the hybrid model?

    • A As a temporary trend

  5. 50. How many days a week do hybrid employees typically go to the office, based on the text?

B2

Adapting to Climate Change

Cities around the world are beginning to adapt to the effects of climate change, rather than only trying to prevent it. In coastal areas, engineers are designing flood barriers and elevated buildings to cope with rising sea levels. In hotter regions, urban planners are adding more green spaces to reduce temperatures and improve air quality. Critics argue that adaptation alone is not enough, and that governments must also invest heavily in reducing emissions. Still, most experts agree that a combination of prevention and adaptation offers the best chance of managing the challenges ahead.

  1. 51. What are engineers doing in coastal areas, according to the passage?

  3. 53. What do critics argue in the passage?

  4. 54. What do most experts agree offers the best chance of managing climate challenges?
